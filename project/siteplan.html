Explore Cebu Site Plan

Site Name

Explore Cebu

The name Explore Cebu represents a travel website that introduces visitors to Cebu's beautiful beaches, waterfalls, mountains, historical landmarks, and local cuisine.

Optional Domain: explorecebu.com

Site Purpose

Explore Cebu is a travel guide website that provides information about tourist attractions, local food, travel tips, and must-visit destinations in Cebu. The goal is to help visitors discover the island and plan an enjoyable trip.

Target Audience

  • Local travelers
  • International tourists
  • Students
  • Families planning vacations

Scenarios

  • Which beaches in Cebu are best for a weekend vacation?
  • What famous foods should I try when visiting Cebu?
